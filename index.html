Our Story

Located in the heart of Kigali, Habesha Ethiopian Restaurant offers a genuine Ethiopian dining experience, bringing together traditional recipes, fresh ingredients, and warm hospitality. Whether you're enjoying our famous Kitfo, The Buja Special, or experiencing our traditional Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony, every visit is made to feel special.
